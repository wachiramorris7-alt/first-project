What is our main aim when we say we are automating homes for sustainability & resource management?


Residential homes face multiple isolated challenges, including high energy consumption, security vulnerabilities, manual waste sorting inefficiencies, wastewater loss, and environmental safety hazards. Traditional single-purpose home automation systems focus on isolated features, failing to provide an integrated, resource-circular approach. This project addresses the integration of automated security, renewable energy management, automated waste sorting, closed-loop water purification, precision agriculture (greenhouse management), and hazard mitigation into a single, unified control architecture. By bridging these domains, the system minimizes a household's environmental footprint while maximizing safety, reliability, and comfort.
